## What this drill does and does not prove

**Proves:** the encrypted base backup decrypts and extracts; the cluster starts
and completes archive recovery; transactions committed *after* the backup are
recovered from archived WAL; RLS remains FORCED and cross-tenant reads are still
denied on the restored cluster; migration history and the audit chain survive.

**Does not prove:** recovery from off-host storage alone (this drill reads the
local WAL archive — the off-host copy is exercised by `backup.sh`, not here);
recovery on different hardware; application-level recovery beyond the database;
or Keycloak realm restore, which is exported but not yet drilled.

**RPO caveat, stated honestly:** this drill forces a WAL switch before the
simulated failure. A real crash does not. Between segment switches the exposure
is bounded by `archive_timeout` (currently 5 minutes) — that is the true
worst-case RPO, and it meets `1.txt` §29 by design rather than by measurement.
