## 🔴 THE TWO RULES THIS SESSION PAID FOR

1. **A POLICY AND A GRANT ARE TWO HALVES OF ONE CONTROL.** An RLS policy
   constrains the columns it names and is INDIFFERENT to the rest of the row.
   `enquiries_public_insert` pins `(product_id, tenant_id, organization_id)`;
   with `GRANT INSERT` the app role could still forge the premium, the currency
   and the product name. **Ask of every policy: what does the GRANT permit that
   the policy does not mention?**

2. **`INSERT ... RETURNING` IS A READ, AND RLS ADJUDICATES IT.** An anonymous
   writer that cannot SELECT its own row cannot use RETURNING. It passes locally
   (definer runs as the superuser owner) and fails on Render, and the error —
   *"new row violates row-level security policy"* — points at the INSERT policy,
   which is innocent.
